# Timeline

This file is intentionally coarse until research narrows dates.

## Phase 0 — Founder ecology
Approx. 80–78 Ma onward, working range.

A small-to-medium Appalachian dromaeosaur-grade population lives in the northern Mississippi Embayment region. It is a mesopredator in a wet coastal-plain / river / floodplain / nearshore mosaic.

## Phase 1 — The Harvest
Repeated use of naturally fermented fruit and other altered-state resources creates recurring seasonal aggregation and stronger social learning.

## Phase 2 — Early Alchul contact
A subset of individuals repeatedly experiences anomalous perception associated with altered states, place, and specific geological conditions.

## Phase 3 — Cultural divergence
Behavior begins to persist across generations through observation, ritual, chemical signaling, memory systems, and group practice.

## Phase 4 — Technical culture
Controlled fire, fermentation, preservation, chemistry, mineral use, containers, traps, hunting systems, and increasingly deliberate experimentation develop over deep time.

## Phase 5 — Perception of displacement
Some individuals learn to perceive local temporal / spatial displacement and aspects of the "other side." These abilities are gradually systematized.

## Phase 6 — The descent
Knowledge of the coming planetary catastrophe changes civilization. Migration underground occurs over very long periods and in many waves.

## Phase 7 — K–Pg surface catastrophe
The surface civilization ends. The subterranean civilization survives.

## Phase 8 — Deep-time divergence
Over ~66 million years the descendants diversify, self-modify, spread through the deep Earth, and develop technologies based on ecosystems, geology, matter-state control, collective cognition, and non-local displacement.

## Phase 9 — Present
The New Madrid population persists as part of a broader Type ~1.7-equivalent civilization. Its visible surface signature is subtle and often mistaken for natural geology.
