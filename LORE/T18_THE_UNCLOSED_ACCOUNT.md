# T18 — The Unclosed Account

**Status:** exploratory ALCHUL worldbuilding, mature pre–K–Pg surface setting. Not a recovered history or newly locked species anatomy. Builds on `CANON.md`, `ALCHUL.md`, `CIVILIZATION.md`, `LORE/EMBODIED_WORLDVIEW_ATLAS.md` and the T17 physical sensing prototype.

## The thing they do not agree about

A place is never merely a point in space to these people. A particular river bend can be known by its wet-clay pressure, the pattern a tail can feel through a support, the slow arrival of a ferment's scent, the return of a prey trail and the traces of those who cared for it. The place is also a relationship. When one inhabitant says, in our translation, *the bank is wrong*, the word "wrong" may mean unstable sediment, a broken civic obligation, a remembered flood pattern, or a felt absence of something that has no settled name.

By the mature surface period they have learned to keep **three different accounts of one encounter**:

1. **The Trace — what happened outside anyone's mind.** The water increased, a reed bent, a clay mark moved, a support sang. Another body could return and find a changed material arrangement.
2. **The Reading — what someone makes of the trace.** One resident interprets the low vibration as an incoming river pulse; another hears ordinary wood strain. Both judgments are shaped by memory, position, training and fear.
3. **The Unclosed Account — the felt contact that will not fit either explanation.** At the Harvest a witness may experience a familiar river in another condition, or a place held strangely outside its own moment. This is not an image of Alchul, an order from Alchul or the proof of a forecast. Alchul remains distinct from the inhabitants; the earliest perceptual opening works in both directions. What Alchul sees, wants or is remains unknown.

The categories do not simply divide science from faith. They are **different obligations**. A trace must be made repeatable when possible. A reading must name the risk of being wrong. An unclosed account must be remembered without letting its keeper command everyone else's actions.

## Who belongs in the scene

These are **narrative functions, not new species, sexes, castes or permanent ranks**. Any adult could learn several, and any younger inhabitant could contradict an elder.

- **The River Listener:** patient, physically attuned, gifted at distinguishing channel noise from deep bank strain. Strength: first warning. Temptation: treating exceptional sensitivity as certainty.
- **The Reed Turner:** an experimentalist who works with water, weighted reed gates, mineralized clay and a suspended stave that can hold a trace after everyone leaves. Strength: reproducibility. Temptation: discounting a witness because an instrument does not yet have a category.
- **The Nursery Keeper:** thinks of a route as shared breathing space, a sequence through which developing eggs and food must move without panic. Strength: keeping all bodies safe. Temptation: choosing a comforting account over an ugly explanation.
- **The Harvest Witness:** can sometimes report the same location under another condition in altered perception. Strength: preserving a rare experience. Temptation: imagining that accurate warning establishes motive or authority.

The characters are **not fixed matches to T16 E1/E2/E3 morphology**. Their anatomy can be illustrated by provisional models, but jobs, social identity and cognitive capability are not derived from model color, stature or tail length.

## A small civic invention

When two readers disagree, they leave two physically separate marks in the same record bed. Neither is permitted to erase the other. A third individual may reproduce a test without being told which sign came from whom.

Their archive is not a row of human books. It might be a shallow clay basin with three exchangeable reeds: one reed carries the pattern of **the thing observed**, another the bodily pattern of **the observer's reading**, and a third—the lightest and most removable—keeps **the account that cannot yet be tested**. This is an **invented visual design motif**, not a claim that all communities have standardized reeds or writing.

For young learners, disagreement is not an embarrassment. It is a place left wide enough for another learner to enter.

## Story promise and boundary

The audience must be allowed to wonder whether an anomalous experience is a subtle ordinary cue, a coincidence, a distorted memory, or genuinely Alchul-linked contact. Within ALCHUL, Alchul is real. **A particular witness can still be mistaken.** No character gets a direct camera view into Alchul or a supernatural answer key.

T18 establishes a durable worldbuilding distinction and a social institution: **the Unclosed Account**, a protected record of testimony that cannot yet claim public certainty. It does not close the mystery.
