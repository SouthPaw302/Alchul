# T20 — The Two Readings at the River Gate

**Status:** exploratory worldbuilding incident and replayable experiment for the late surface civilization, not archaeological history. Companion to T18's Unclosed Account and T19's Language of Weights.

## The disagreement

At the Braided Reach's northern station, a low shiver runs through a load-bearing reed gate just before the afternoon water begins to rise. The River Listener feels a change through the inner support. The Reed Turner hears a different frequency, one that could come from ordinary damp wood adjusting to heat. They both stop others from stepping onto the soft bank. They **do not** agree about why.

Beside them is a modest instrument: a mineral-clay bed with three reed staves that can be swapped without losing their length and the weight hung at each end. It is not an electronic sensor or a primitive computer. It is a deliberately arranged part of the world. The Listener calls a change. The Turner asks for a second reading. The Keeper closes only the narrow route and keeps the broad path open.

A fourth resident, returning from the Harvest, reports an **Absent Moment**: the same river bend seeming briefly empty of the water it now contains. The claim is recorded in the third reed without taking precedence over the two physical readings. There is no visible Alchul presence, command or explanatory apparition.

## Replay sequence

1. **Read without being told.** The Listener and Turner each register one of three interpretations: *bank pulse*, *wood strain*, or *undetermined*. The player's first choice is not shown to the other observer.
2. **Make a small alteration.** Move the load from one reed support and leave the water supply unchanged. If the anomalous vibration follows the support, ordinary structural stress becomes more plausible.
3. **Make a second alteration.** Open a parallel flow channel and preserve the support. If the pulse changes with the water path, a river-origin explanation becomes more plausible.
4. **Repeat blind.** An assistant resets the instrument without telling either observer whether the load or flow was altered. Both repeat their readings and have them recorded independently.
5. **Compare traces, not prestige.** The two records can converge, remain opposed, or show that the experiment was too noisy. The Keeper acts on the material safety risk regardless of whose reading was better.
6. **Preserve a different kind of account.** The Harvest witness is invited to place their report in the third reed, marked as unclosed; their experience is neither disproved nor promoted to a physical signal.

## Three branching endings

### Ending A — The river answered

The second test changes when water is redirected, and the repeated blind reading follows it. The Listener was close to the cause. They do **not** take a victory posture. They move the clay weights, then ask the Turner to carry the new arrangement to another bank. A good reading becomes useful only when someone else can work with it.

### Ending B — The support answered

The apparent pulse tracks a shift in the weighted stave, not the water. The Turner was close to the cause. The Listener's body may have sensed a real change but assigned it the wrong origin. Their perception was valuable; their interpretation was incomplete. That distinction becomes the first lesson handed to an apprentice.

### Ending C — The account stays unclosed

A noisy signal persists under both alterations. Each observer keeps a separate explanation. The group does not rename uncertainty as prophecy; nor does it banish the witness's experience as dishonesty. They close the narrow route for the day, leave a new record bed beside the gate and agree to return with a different instrument when the temperature has changed.

## What the culture learns

The turning point is not when everybody agrees. It is when they can **act together without agreeing about every cause**. This is a plausible seed of both their technical sophistication and their richly entwined sacred-scientific practices. Their future machines might be ecosystems, but the social discovery begins with a weighted reed, a dangerous bank, a disputed witness and a narrow route left open to correction.

The scene can be replayed with different noise and observation positions. No ending certifies that contact with Alchul occurred in that particular incident, although contact is real elsewhere in ALCHUL canon. Nothing here dictates their eventual language, physiology or complete civic system.
