# ALCHUL — An Indigenous Technological Civilization, Developed From Within

**Creator direction: 2026-11-23.** This is the active authoring rule for early, middle and late **surface-era** worldbuilding and for T21 onward. It clarifies existing canon rather than naming a new force or replacing the Harvest. We are creating a world, **not claiming to recover an otherwise unknown human or dinosaur history**.

## 1. Whose achievement is this?

The descendants of the Late Cretaceous Appalachian theropod founder population develop intelligence, social complexity, practical knowledge and technology **on their own evolutionary and cultural trajectory**. Like human communities, they learn through ecological opportunity, error, competition, cooperation, memory, accidental discovery, imitation, improvisation, teaching, deliberate trials and long periods of cumulative cultural change.

For every surface-era technology we develop, the working explanation must stand even if the author momentarily removes Alchul from the story. **No technological milestone depends on a dream transmitting a blueprint, an invisible benefactor moving a stone, revelation of a chemical recipe, an other-side actuator, or an unexplained jump in intelligence.** Their biological material sensitivities can be ordinary evolved capacities refined by training, instrument design and learned interpretation. Tools extend perception; improved perception leads to better tools. Neither requires divine engineering.

This does **not** retcon Alchul's in-universe existence or the later possibility that he influences experiences. It is a **rule of causal narration**: when developing this civilization, first tell the complete material, biological, social and engineering story from the inhabitants' perspective.

## 2. The background presence: faint, culturally interpreted, non-commanding

The Harvest begins with **real natural fermentation**. The material attracts repeated gatherings, providing nutrition, altered states and new social opportunities. Some inhabitants subsequently have dreams, repeating impressions, startling intuitions and experiences of a familiar place under another condition. In the broader canon, these altered states permit an early perceptual opening involving Alchul in both directions.

**At this period, their awareness of Alchul is extremely indistinct**—more comparable to humanity's many accounts of dreams, archetypes, inspiration, belief and the unexplained than a recognized personal visitor. Different individuals and communities make different meanings from these experiences; none possesses an authoritative account of his true identity, nature, desires or physical mechanism.

Their distant future—**our story's present day, after enormous evolutionary, geological and cultural intervals**—may include a much more developed awareness of Alchul's *real existence*. That future relationship is not a sensible shortcut for the minds, equipment or social organization of their remote surface ancestors. Increasing awareness does not imply complete understanding, a single unified theology or the authority to explain Alchul.

We will therefore **leave Alchul offstage** throughout the current technology-development run. Dreams may appear later as occasional ambiguous cultural experience, without supplying technical designs or serving as evidence for why a device works.

## 3. What we mean by science within this world

The inhabitants need not invent laboratories, journals or human philosophical categories at the same point that humans did. They can still possess **repeatable practical knowledge**: a support carries weight or fails; a drying material preserves food or spoils; diverted water alters a bank; the same heated mineral cracks at similar seams; a ferment changes over time; a different observer can repeat the procedure.

For authoring, distinguish **observed process**, **character explanation** and **authorial speculation**. A scientific theory is not merely an untested guess: real-world theories are explanatory frameworks constrained by evidence and open to revision. Our fictional civilization's models can be wrong, incomplete or culturally disputed while their inventions still work.

We can exercise **bounded artistic freedom** over alien social customs, exact cultural sequence, sensory priorities, inhabited places, instruments and materials. We should not claim measured fossil organs, exact unrecorded chronology, or improbable matter manipulation as established real-world facts. These are our coherent inventions.

## 4. A long, branching technology-development map

These are **working development bands**, not fixed archaeological dates, a requirement that every group passes through identical stages, or a replacement for `TIMELINE.md`. Many tracks overlap; populations exchange knowledge unevenly and sometimes lose it.

| **A. Attention and pursuit** | Dangerous prey, flood edges, seasonality and competition | Collective tracking cues, body-oriented signaling, repeated routes, cooperative ambush and refuge-memory | Young learn the difference between a remembered pattern and a current signal |
| **B. Returning grounds** | Seasonal fermented resources, food safety, social toleration | Food caches, controlled access, selection and transport of ripe plants, observational gathering cycles | Comparing batches, sites, symptoms and successful return times |
| **C. Preservation and shelter** | Spoilage, nesting exposure, heat and moisture variation | Smoke and drying practices, shaded storage, woven organic containers, raised nursery margins, rot-resistant structures | Better handling of airflow, drainage, fire and biological contamination |
| **D. Fire, culture and vessels** | Repeatable heat, food preparation, care and chemistry | Controlled fuel, mineral-clay vessels, low-temperature processes, intentional managed fermentation | Temperature comparison, sequencing, sealed and vented containers, shared process memory |
| **E. Reading material weakness** | Fracturing banks, unstable supports, useful stone and wood | Reading vibration, sound, cracks, leakage, thermal gradients; controlled wedges and strain relief | From intuition to comparison marks, proxy gauges and independent inspection |
| **F. **Not human Iron Age by default.** Their path branches around water, mineral stress, cultivated biology and chemistry rather than repeating a compulsory human sequence of bronze, iron, coal and mass factories. Isolated metal use is not automatically excluded, but it is not the engine of this technological arc.

## 5. The standard invention record for T21 onward

For each new craft, structure or daily-life system, our story work will answer six questions:

1. **Why would they need it?** A concrete survival, ecological, care, social or material-processing problem.
2. **What could their bodies notice and manipulate?** A function plausible for their then-current unapproved theropod-descendant body plan; no effortless human hand motion.
3. **What is the material mechanism?** Force, pressure, heat, chemical conversion, motion, living growth, maintenance, stress relief or other causally described process.
4. **How was it learned?** Accident, apprenticeship, unsuccessful trials, comparison between habitats, multiple inventors and long-term transmission.
5. **What fails or costs something?** Leakage, contamination, illness, water competition, exhaust, injury, reproductive burden, lost habitat, conflict or a flawed reading.
6. **What grows from it next?** An improved instrument, a new dependency, a civic obligation or a future craft.

An invention passes the **narrative independence test** if we can write all six answers with no Alchul cameo or implied supernatural assistance. Such a craft may later acquire religious associations, but those are cultural interpretations, not engineering specifications.

## 6. First detailed T21 workstream: one genuinely living settlement

Rather than starting with a lore-heavy explanation of advanced capabilities, build a **river-margin settlement as interdependent processes**:

- **Flood and route system:** river readings, seasonal banks, load-bearing roots and reed gates, diversion and escape paths.
- **Food and fermentation:** gathering, sorting, species-safe processing, contamination, storage and long-term culture management.
- **Nursery and bodies:** safe air and thermal gradients, vulnerable routes, internally carried eggs through roughly three quarters of **development**, and protected external incubation. The reproductive anatomy and oxygen-transfer details remain artistic/scientific reconstruction questions.
- **Material and heat workshops:** fuel, clean combustion, mineral/wood joints, wedging, cooling, fracture, pressure, wear and repair.
- **Shared methods and culture:** learned gestures, different skill sets, practical comparison marks, mistakes remembered, disagreement resolved enough to act together.
- **Ordinary living:** hunting or food exchange, companions, apprenticeship, conflict, play, care, injury, environmental stewardship and festivals. The inhabitants must feel like **people living in their world** rather than students operating our science instruments.

**T21's driving question:** What does a normal resident have to do tomorrow to keep this place alive—and what technology exists because their ancestors confronted that task for a very long time?

## 7. Era control

Do not collapse early Harvest, mature surface, first below, K–Pg, 66 million years of deep evolution and the story's present into one technological or anatomical moment. The later civilization's greater awareness of Alchul and far more advanced material/boundary technologies must be earned across those vast intervals, not projected backwards.

**Authorial rule for current work: independent civilization first; exceptional contact in the background; no external-force explanation for invention.**
