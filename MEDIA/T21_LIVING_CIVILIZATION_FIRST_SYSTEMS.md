# ALCHUL T21 — The Living Civilization: Seven Responsibilities and One Bank

**Authoring date:** 2026-11-24. **Scope:** late surface era, before the Long Descent. **Status:** provisional fictional engineering/cultural reconstruction; 3D scene is a dimensionless interactive study, not a recovered archaeological site, verified fossil sensory apparatus, calibrated hydraulics, or finalized body design.

## 1. The problem that makes a civilization visible

A side channel at the Braided Reach runs beneath a cultivated root shelf. The shelf supports a protected nursery route and shares its clean-water intake with the fermentation/food grounds. A mineral-and-wood workshop draws water from a separate return channel. The river rises; fine sediment enters the source; retained heat and decay create gas beneath a covered material bed. All four places must survive the same day.

**The inhabitants detect environmental differences rather than command matter.** Their evolved senses and learned instruments provide imperfect, repeatable clues. The first act is to listen to the bank, not to move a boulder by mind. Engineering comes from the measured weakness of a material: a wedge at an existing seam, a controlled vent, a screen, a pressure diversion, deliberate heat storage, a repaired drainage path.

### An interdependent daily system, not a museum of props

| Responsibility | What it needs | What it supplies | What it can damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| River readers | access to bank, learning records, time | advance warning and a tested choice of relief route | an incorrect flood reading can cost the nursery |
| Material workers | spare wood, patient survey, water and ventilation | braces, wedges, repairs, pressure experiments | an over-strong brace can raise local backpressure |
| Water keepers | filtered intake, cleaning fiber, channel maintenance | cleaner drinking/nursery water | reduced flow can starve fermentation beds |
| Culture keepers | substrate, clean water, air, guarded batches | preserved food, tested microbial cultures, useful processing by-products | spoiled batches and contamination can spread |
| Nursery keepers | thermal mass, clean routes, temperate air and water | safe conditions for the final external incubation period | gas accumulation, heat loss, evacuation bottlenecks |
| Movers/hunters | safe paths, shared labor and access to prey/forest | materials and food; expedient evacuation | delayed maintenance and vulnerable bodies at risk |
| Apprentices/recorders | observed repetitions, diverse elders and a willingness to admit mistakes | long-term retention of failed and successful interventions | confidently copying an untested interpretation |

No role is a permanent caste; none requires a human body. The short English names translate complex social and sensory identities for readers only.

## 2. Seven invention records with the six-question independence method

### A — The listening stave and test notch

1. **Need:** find pressure changes and unstable supports before an overt flood.
2. **Affordance:** a trained reader compares foot-borne substrate vibration and sound along a handled stave or root.
3. **Mechanism:** mechanical oscillations travel through different densities, seams and wetted structures; material/position changes the signal.
4. **Learning:** two readers take separate measurements, adjust the stave position and archive mismatches.
5. **Failure:** sound from wet wood resembles movement in the bank; false alarms and false reassurance are both possible.
6. **Successor:** more controlled stake placement, paired measurements, independent record beds.

### B — The relieved pressure cut

1. **Need:** keep an overburdened bank from failing toward habitation.
2. **Affordance:** inherited terrain attention plus tested knowledge of where the channel flows under different loads.
3. **Mechanism:** a deliberately lowered diversion path releases flow through a less dangerous route before it breaks the inhabited shelf.
4. **Learning:** seasonal diversions, smaller practice cuts and the memory of costly failures.
5. **Failure:** the lower channel passes through a valuable culture garden; loss of food is intentional, not an accident without consequence.
6. **Successor:** sacrificial flood plains, protected stores, more effective sluice/repair pathways.

### C — A seam brace

1. **Need:** slow local structural failure until evacuations and repairs finish.
2. **Affordance:** body-weight bracing, shaped wedged timber, coordinated several-body placement.
3. **Mechanism:** a loaded support transfers force across an existing discontinuity.
4. **Learning:** mark what the support carried before it broke; compare with neighboring joints.
5. **Failure:** the local brace can choke flow and produce higher pressure elsewhere.
6. **Successor:** a shared map of support loads and sacrificial rather than rigid joints.

### D — The screened intake

1. **Need:** protect fermentation food and nursery water from newly mobilized sediment and decaying matter.
2. **Affordance:** recurrent work with reeds, fiber, roots, settling pools and visual/chemical discrimination.
3. **Mechanism:** particulate settling and physical filtration; chemical purity is not implied by visual clarity.
4. **Learning:** repeated side-by-side batch keeping; a spoiled stock becomes a remembered sample, not a sacred mistake.
5. **Failure:** screened flow is slower and may shrink available water to the culture grounds.
6. **Successor:** layered filters, distributed reservoirs, independently protected water supply.

### E — Gas venting

1. **Need:** prevent dangerous gas pockets in covered warming/material chambers.
2. **Affordance:** odor sensitivity when applicable, displacement of air, attention to animal behavior and careful opening from safe routes.
3. **Mechanism:** a vent alters airflow through a confined space.
4. **Learning:** measurements of air movement before/after and recorded failures, avoiding claims of universal gas smell (some gases are odorless).
5. **Failure:** venting also loses stored warmth and changes humidity.
6. **Successor:** separate exhaust channels, baffles, non-reliance on odor alone.

### F — Nursery thermal mass

1. **Need:** stabilize a short but vulnerable external egg incubation period after approximately three quarters of development is retained internally.
2. **Affordance:** controlled fire, warmed minerals and insulation from a long-established shelter craft.
3. **Mechanism:** thermal storage and gradual heat release; no unsupported specific reproductive organs.
4. **Learning:** compare successful clutches and marked heating cycles across repeated years.
5. **Failure:** excess heat, exhaust, gas or dried air can be worse than modest cold.
6. **Successor:** isolated warm rooms, separate ventilation, low-temperature material systems.

### G — Food rescue and protected pathways

1. **Need:** preserve cultivated stores without endangering residents in the flood path.
2. **Affordance:** coordinated carriers, elevated routes, movable frames and practiced signals.
3. **Mechanism:** goods relocated to higher/drier shelter; routes are physically inspected before carriers enter.
4. **Learning:** drills, mistakes observed by juveniles, and route marks maintained by many hands/feet.
5. **Failure:** moving food steals labor from bracing and nursery protection.
6. **Successor:** emergency stock separation and redundancy across settlements.

## 3. A playable trade-off model

The Godot **T21 Day of Small Decisions** is a first linked systems study. Each choice costs one watch; flooding, gas accumulation, cooling and food consumption continue while the residents act. A brace improves bank integrity but adds backpressure. Relief reduces pressure but deliberately sacrifices culture grounds. A screen improves water clarity but interrupts culture water. Ventilation reduces gas but loses nursery warmth. Heating restores warmth at a material cost but increases exhaust risk. Relocating food consumes valuable labor and materials.

Variables are dimensionless and explicitly **fictional**, not historical quantities or scientific estimates. A successful ending requires all critical systems to remain inside authored safety bands, including an explicitly checked and protected nursery route. The player cannot solve every problem without sacrifices. The scene contains **no Alchul control button**, no paranormal actuation, no species-grade sensor receptor and no historical claims.
