# ALCHUL — Batch 11: Inhabitants of the Mature Surface Civilization

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** SCIENTIFIC DESIGN STUDY / NOT CANON / NOT A FINISHED 3D MODEL  
**Historical moment:** peak surface era in the Late Cretaceous, **before** sustained Long Descent. The absolute year is unresolved.  
**Site context:** Braided Reach–type river margin, a *fictional working settlement*, not a proven locality.  
**Production priority:** world history and biology. Desktop app development is parked as an optional presentation medium, **not** a dependency for historical reconstruction.

## 1. We are here in the history

The studied line of descent is:
1. Unnamed feathered Appalachian maniraptoran/dromaeosaur-grade hunter lineage, a working founding window ~80–78 Ma.
2. Naturally recurring Harvest → individual altered experiences → increasing aggregation, learning and cultural persistence.
3. Technical emergence: preservation, fire, fermentation, mechanical/mineral work, water, pressure, chemistry, sensory method, nonhuman institutions.
4. **Our current reconstruction: the last several millennia of mature surface civilization preceding sustained deep migration.** They are not founders and have already undergone substantial biological/cultural change.
5. Later: controversial evidence of approaching surface catastrophe, progressive and uneven Long Descent, K–Pg survival, many millions of years of deep adaptation and directed diversity, present New Madrid and other worlds.

The game is a representation, not a measure of how much of the civilization's history has been researched. Our 12 development *media* batches do **not** mean the total universe is nearly complete.

## 2. Locked boundaries

From `CANON.md`, `SPECIES.md`, `VISUAL_CANON.md`, `TIMELINE.md`:
- Founders are Earth-born, feathered, predatory theropods, not humans, generic movie raptors or modern birds.
- Surface descendants' anatomy must follow evolutionary causation; they are not simply unchanged founding animals. **No final mature body mass, stature, skull, wrist range, tail or respiratory map is locked.**
- By mature surface time, egg-bearing descendants retain embryos internally for ~**75% of development**, then lay eggs for the remaining incubation. The reproductive physiology is unresolved; do not invent a mammalian womb or display a precise organ system as fact.
- Mature surface civilization never follows a human Iron Age. The dominant technical path cultivates environmental and material processes.
- Specialized sensitivity to real geological cues involves vibration, water, heat, stress, gas and material failure, not telekinetic stone levitation.
- Alchul is real in-universe but its identity, shape, purpose and viewpoint **remain unknown**.
- Surface setting and climate must reflect a changing Late Cretaceous northern Embayment, **not** modern Mississippi scenery.

## 4. Two deliberately competing **adult peak-surface** hypotheses

Neither is an approved species definition, and neither is identical to the founder.

### H-A — Continuity-biased ground reader
- Long-to-moderate muscular feather-derived tail remains important for counterbalance and communication.
- Forward-held neck and moderately horizontal torso; pelvis carries principal mass over digitigrade hindlimbs.
- Three-digit hands adapted to brace and align tools, but forelimb reach/rotation remains limited by derived theropod wrist mechanics.
- Observational behaviors: long stable pauses while reading a riverbank; tail adjustment before pivot, low contact points and highly precise foot placement.
- **Prediction to test:** a longer counterbalancing tail reduces necessary compensatory upper-body movement in a forward, loaded grasping pose; requires more corridor clearance.
- **Potential failure:** if an ergonomically useful three-digit work envelope demands unsustainable forward reach or large lateral clearance, this morphology may not explain their technology and inhabited architecture well.

### H-B — Manipulation-biased upright reader
- Descendant retains theropod hindlimbs and clearly nonmammalian pelvis, but tail length/mass are reduced relative to H-A; redistribution of torso/neck mass supports more stable hand work nearer the trunk.
- Flexible raised anterior posture *without* human shoulders, chest, primate palm or knees.
- Forelimbs emphasize non-primate three-contact support and repeated precision placement; hand movement still restricted by tested joint axes.
- Observational behaviors: small alternating balance shifts while precisely placing a wooden/sedimentary calibration tool; a widened supporting stance; more compact turning at shared workstations.
- **Prediction to test:** shorter posterior clearance helps route sharing, but increased load or precision work could demand more ankle/hip stabilization and a different center-of-mass envelope.
- **Potential failure:** if a candidate forelimb pose only works with prohibited human wrist pronation, or stability requires a mammalian torso, the hypothesis is rejected or redesigned.

Both models must coexist on a comparison plate before a preference is chosen; Tesh remains an individual study, not the species template. No precise mass, height, tail ratio, joint degree limits or behavior frequency is asserted until tested.

## 5. Reproduction is a systemic design constraint

The ~75% internal development is an in-universe premise; it places higher demands on:
- oxygen transfer before shell deposition or through a suitably permeable developing shell;
- adult energetic burden, carrying loads, gait, substrate hazard and resting postures;
- post-laying final incubation, including moisture, contamination, gas exchange and thermal gradients;
- alternate safe nursery paths, time spent at care sites and pooled community work.

**Working tests (not anatomical facts):** model a plausible *envelope* of changed carrying mass without selecting a clutch count; compare traversability and work-lane widths between ordinary and egg-bearing adults; keep exact shell and developmental mechanism open. Label every image and animation accordingly.
