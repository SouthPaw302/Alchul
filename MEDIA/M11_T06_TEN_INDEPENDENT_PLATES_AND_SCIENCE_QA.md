## 2. Scientific integrity and rejection flags

The plates illustrate two competing *fictional* evolutionary design options, **not** measured organism designs. Several AI-authored labels on the pictures introduce **false precision**. Do not treat any pictured centimeter/metre scale, body mass, exact tail ratio, foot pressure, COM position, stride efficiency, turning radius, passage width, wrist angle, confidence interval or experimental percentage as data. These were not computed or independently verified. They are not measurements of extinct taxa or the fictional descendants. Some generated text may also be typographically incorrect.

Additional unresolved labels:
- A strongly keratinized or modern birdlike beak is an illustrative design choice, **not** known mature-surface anatomy.
- The depicted wrist and digits have **not** passed skeleton, tendon, joint-axis, contact-force or opposition tests. Prohibition of free mammal-style pronation remains.
- Regional plumage and sensory capabilities are comparative inspirations only. A modern crocodilian receptor analogue cannot be asserted as a fossil trait of a derived theropod.
- The approximately **75% internal egg-development period is in-universe canon**, but the shell, oxygen transfer, carrying load, clutch, reproductive tract and nursery clearances are unresolved.
- Apparent experimental findings, observers' confidence percentages, or mechanical results on plates are **invented illustrative storytelling**, not measurements of an executed protocol.
- The two full lateral designs have not been independently redrawn at a verifiably identical spatial datum. The separate Godot M11 lab offers a common proxy datum but is not a validated skeleton.
