## Controls

Open `godot/scenes/t22_era_atlas.tscn` in Godot. Select one of five eras; Earth-only mode is default. Disable it to see the separately labeled fictional overlay. Only mature surface has a runnable study. Open it, use original 1–8 interventions, N after a successful day, then select two distinct duties each day using 1–6. M toggles original study audio. After the eight days J/K/L record one recommendation (share record / prioritize repair / repeat study); this never edits history. R restarts. Other eras explicitly have no runnable scenes.

The integrated view uses original M2 plus the prior procedural terrace. Six bounded visual markers per resource are proxies, not anatomical inhabitants, numerical hydraulic simulations, real crop species or population counts. Audio is a two-second synthesized tonal bed, not a species language or recording. T21.4–9 are intentionally scoped prototypes; full final art and relationships are not completed.

## T22 boundary contract

The T12 nine-period Earth timeline remains independently available at `godot/data/m11_t12_real_earth_timeline.json`. The five fictional eras are an additional registry, not a replacement for geological time. First Below and Long Descent are ordered story stages with open dates. 66 Ma is the real K–Pg boundary; hidden survival of non-avian descendants is counterfactual fiction. The present New Madrid zone is intraplate within the North American plate, and the modern hidden civilization is fictional. No modern fault is assumed an ancient entrance. No old terrain, speculative body, reproductive trait or technology is inherited by default.

Unknown eras/features are rejected; globally denied claims cannot be enabled by era allowlists. Unbuilt eras return an empty scene path. This gate controls the new atlas, not every preexisting research scene in the repository. The mature surface scene is not promoted into Chapter I.
