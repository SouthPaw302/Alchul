## 3. Evidence policy

**Locked in-universe constraints:** Earth-derived Appalachian feathered maniraptoran ancestors; mature surface era before the Long Descent; natural-geological process engineering rather than an Iron Age; circa 75% internal egg development; Alchul's identity unshown. Governed by `CANON.md`.

**Comparative evidence:** Published work confirms feathers in at least some dromaeosaurids and a complex relationship between shape and COM in bird-line archosaurs. Studies of extant reptiles establish oxygen delivery as a critical challenge to prolonged egg retention, **not** a proven mechanism for our fictional theropod descendants.

**Illustrated hypotheses only:** Exact body ratios, coloring, crest, skull, tail reduction, joint ranges, maternal body shape, sensory organs, cultural roles and named individuals. None are approved canon.

References: [Turner et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17885130/); [Macaulay et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37317-y); [Andrews and Mathies, 2000s, reptilian oxygen constraint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12024290/).
