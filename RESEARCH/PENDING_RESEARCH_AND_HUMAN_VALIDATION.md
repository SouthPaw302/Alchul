## 3. Additional premise research: technological intelligence and deep-time detectability

Deferred, not a prerequisite to writing:

- Compare fossil-supported theropod cognition/manipulation hypotheses with demonstrated tool use, cultural learning and engineering in hominins, apes, corvids/parrots, cetaceans, cephalopods and social insects. Distinguish traits or behaviors from demonstrated industrial civilization; humans are the only known industrial civilization.
- Review Schmidt & Frank, [The Silurian hypothesis: would it be possible to detect an industrial civilization in the geological record?](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1473550418000095). Its detectability question is relevant; it is not evidence of an earlier civilization.
- Examine taphonomy, erosion, burial, mineralization, durable artifacts, chemical/isotopic anomalies, ecological disturbance and energy use. Organic or natural-system-based technology may leave different signatures from industrial technology, but invisibility is not guaranteed.
- Explore convergence and independent origins of intelligence as plausibility constraints, not as proof of the fictional lineage.
