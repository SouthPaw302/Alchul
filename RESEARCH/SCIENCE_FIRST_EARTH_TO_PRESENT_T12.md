# Science-first Earth, Late Cretaceous through the present — T12 foundation

**Prepared:** 2026-11-23. **Scope:** northern Mississippi Embayment / Reelfoot rift / modern New Madrid region. **Status:** documented real science plus clearly separated fictional overlays. **Source hierarchy:** primary publications and official agencies; working regional inference; explicitly fictional world canon; unknown. This is the standing research prerequisite for later ALCHUL period reconstructions, not a proposed replacement for CANON.md.

## Governing method and non-negotiable truth labels

1. **OBSERVED / PUBLISHED:** rocks, stratigraphy, fossils, instrumental monitoring and well-supported regional correlations, with source and limits.
2. **SCIENTIFIC INFERENCE:** possible habitat or movement consequences consistent with known mechanisms but not verified at an exact ALCHUL site.
3. **FICTIONAL WORLD-CANON:** what is true in ALCHUL's story, even when it contradicts the known fossil record. Never insert it into a measured Earth chronology as data.
4. **NOT RECONSTRUCTED:** scientific or fictional specifics we have not established. Do not generate a canonical anatomy, modern biological species, temperature, size or hidden structure simply to make a scene feel complete.

**Separate historical tracks:** the **real-Earth track runs continuously to 2026** whether or not an ALCHUL scene is ready. The story's hypothetical surface and post-impact population must be constrained at every period *after* the Earth track is built, but is not thereby evidenced. Period tags, sources, provenance, direct/indirect uncertainty, and off-switches are mandatory. Chapter I and H-A/H-B remain isolated studies.

**Geological mechanism correction:** The New Madrid earthquakes are not "a fault not connected to any tectonic plate." It is an intraplate fault network associated with an old failed rift within the North American plate. Old crustal weaknesses may be reactivated by ongoing tectonic stresses. A hidden civilization is never needed to explain the observed Earth science. [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/new-madrid-seismic-zone).

**Time-scale correction:** ~80 Ma, the ~70 Ma working fictional occupancy and the present 2026 calendar are separate dating systems. An invented population date is not evidence for a fossil site. The numerical geological periods use ICS, including the 66.0 Ma K–Pg boundary, 23.03 Ma base Neogene, and 2.58 Ma base Quaternary; revisions remain possible. [ICS](https://stratigraphy.org/chart/).

## Real-world constraints that must drive the *next* development slices

### Fossil and organism constraints
- Establish the range of known Appalachian maniraptoran/dromaeosaur-grade forms; identify which anatomical reconstructions derive from actual specimens and which are phylogenetic comparative hypotheses.
- Infer no direct sexual anatomy, late egg retention, eye types, feathers or grasp from specimens that lack the relevant evidence. The T11 ~75% retention is a locked **fictional reproductive trait**, not established dinosaur biology. [Reptile comparative limits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23348778/).
- Non-avian descendants after 66 Ma belong exclusively to **fictional counterfactual history**. The real-world survival lineage is birds.

### Physical geology / ecology constraints
- Separate rock formation age, fault movement age, modern outcrop position and when a cave could have been open. Do not connect ancient underground entrances to a present New Madrid map without independent work.
- Rebuild each real period's sedimentation, climate, hydrology, biotic communities, resource constraints and feasible subsurface energy/air/water balance **before** overlaying a settlement.
- Honor source-dependent chronological changes to rivers, faults, groundwater, surface habitats and hazards. Never scale one static Godot terrain across 66 million years.

### Present-day scientific constraints
- Modern earthquakes are measured by seismometers, not inferred as evidence for an active hidden city. The NMSZ is an intraplate stress system within North America. Real environmental geology is a standalone learnable layer.
- Contemporary flora, fauna, human land use, and Indigenous cultural histories must be independently researched and protected against retroactive fictional reinterpretations.
- A highly advanced in-universe civilization can be *written* into present-day ALCHUL, but must not be conflated with a supported real-world discovery.

## Required operating workflow (all future T13+ tasks)

1. Choose an **Earth period** and extract observed constraints with citations and uncertainty.
2. Build an era-specific geological/ecological **physical baseline** and mark unknown dimensions. Reject reused terrain when age-specific data invalidate it.
3. Test hypothetical biological adaptations as **separate models**, with negative controls and explicit unknowns.
4. Only then overlay specific ALCHUL canonical or provisional inhabitants, social systems, technology and narrative.
5. Reject false precision: *no* exact settlement coordinates, anatomy, width, gas mixture, reproduction organ or contemporary population without a dedicated evidence/fiction provenance ledger.
6. Keep **both tracks through present-day 2026**. The real Earth history remains valid if every invented layer is hidden.
