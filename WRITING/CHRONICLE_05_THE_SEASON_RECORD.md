# Chronicle 05 — The Season Record

*The Braided Reach, mature surface age. Fictional account; names and indicated meanings are translation conveniences, not approved speech or anatomy.*

The day the bank held was not the day their work ended.

Sen expected the repaired seam to be remembered as a victory. Instead, beside the old listening staves, Vek placed the record of what had been lost: a flooded culture bed, the timbers used for the brace, a carrier's interrupted rest.

*We have borrowed from the next day.*

The words, as we translate them, did not signify debt between individuals. They described the condition of the settlement.

When the waters rose, the nursery keepers requested another inspection of the high route. A pair of learners went with them, carrying a copy of the last survey. One remembered the split stave. The other had never seen it. Sen brought the damaged instrument from the workshop and let them compare it with an intact one.

Later, during the warm growth, Vek refused to assign both trained keepers to the same preparation. One stayed with the nursery workers, who were adapting the shade and draft according to their own observations. The culture grounds ran more slowly. The younger keeper learned to preserve a small starter separately from the main bed. The next preparation would not depend entirely on the survival of the first.

At lean water, the food grounds narrowed. Eru had planned to spend the interval renewing the carry frames. The reserve of preserved food was lower than expected. The work group decided to postpone the frames, but recorded the postponed work rather than pretending the obligation had vanished.

Not every disagreement was resolved by agreement. Sometimes two proposals remained beside each other on the record until a small experiment, or the next change in water, provided grounds to choose.

As the air cooled, the nursery took more of their attention. Some developing young still remained within those carrying them; other, later-stage eggs rested in protected beds. Nobody claimed the needs of one stage were the needs of the other. Keepers adjusted their duties around the individuals who required care. A tired worker relinquished a turn at the warm shelf without having to prove an injury.

Sen found Vek in the quiet interval beside the record shelf.

*Will we have enough for the next rising?*

Vek indicated the empty spaces in the record. The question was not only whether the store would hold. It was whether someone would know what to do when it did not.

Sen brought the younger observer over to explain the damaged stave.

The settlement had many kinds of inheritance.

Some of them had to be taught.

*End of Chronicle 05.*
