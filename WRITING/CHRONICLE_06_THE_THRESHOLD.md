# Chronicle 06 — The Threshold

*Braided Reach, mature surface age; fictional narrative, translated meanings, no approved bodies.*

The younger learners could now read the record without Sen.

They knew why the root bank had been braced, why one preparation had been separated from the rest, and why the nursery's protected route had been inspected even on quiet days. Yet the next rising water had arrived differently. The repair that had held the previous season was not a promise.

Eru brought a piece of worked material from a shallow trial chamber near the settlement. The air had moved differently there. Its opening had remained clear, but the inner surface was damp and the resonance of the support had changed.

A learner asked whether the chamber could shelter the young.

Vek indicated refusal. There was a difference between a place that could be entered and a place that could be inhabited. They had tested the route, not the lives that might depend upon it.

The council recorded three recommendations. Some wanted the measurements shared with neighboring settlements. Others wanted to strengthen the route first. A third group wanted another controlled trial before committing scarce resources. None could erase what the record already showed.

Sen carried the flawed stave beside the new records. He remembered his first false reading, and the difference between a warning and an instrument that needed repair.

The chamber remained empty that season.

They did not yet know how to live beneath the earth. They knew how to ask a better question.
